About

Who writes this, and the fine print

Michael K. Baines spent thirty-nine years in aerospace engineering, where nothing is trusted until it has been checked, and checked again. He brings that habit to larger questions: what the universe is made of, and how connected we really are. He is the author of A Quest for The Big TOE and the new edition of Rediscovering Oneness, and the founder of the Ic² Research Institute. He lives in Bangkok. A fuller biography and press photo are on the press page.

A note on what you’ll read here

The writing on this site, and in the books, is the author’s own work and reflects his views and his reading of the evidence. It is offered for general information and reflection. It is not medical, psychological, financial, legal or other professional advice, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified professional who knows your situation.

Where these pages summarize research, including the health and wellbeing studies discussed on Claims, Seen Clearly, they describe what the studies report. They do not tell you what to do about your own health, mind or money. If you are weighing a decision in any of those areas, please talk to a professional. Links to other websites are offered for convenience and are not endorsements; the author is not responsible for their content.

Your privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. Nothing you do here is followed.

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is held only so the newsletter can be sent to you, through Buttondown, the service that delivers it. It is never sold or shared, and every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.

If you buy a book, the payment and the download are handled by Payhip, which collects what it needs to take the payment and deliver the file under its own privacy terms. To ask what is held about you, or to have it removed, write to mkb.info@proton.me.

Buying the book

Rediscovering Oneness is sold as a PDF through Payhip, which takes the payment and delivers the file as soon as the purchase is complete, with a receipt by email. It is an ordinary PDF and opens in any reader; the book page has notes on reading it on Kindle, having it read aloud, and translating it.

Because it is a digital download that cannot be returned once it has been sent, purchases are final. If anything goes wrong, though, the file will not open, the download fails, or you were charged in error, write to mkb.info@proton.me and it will be put right.
