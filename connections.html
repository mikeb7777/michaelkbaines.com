Connections

The ways we are joined to the world, and the trackers that show how those joins are faring.

The book makes one argument: you are distinct, but you are not separate. Look closely at any edge you can name, your breath, your water, your food, your body, and you find exchange across it, not a wall.

If that is true, then the health of the whole is the condition of your own. Each tracker below reads like a vital sign: where the measure sat in the past, the safe limit, where it stands now, and where it is heading. Most lines run the wrong way. That is the honest picture, and it is also why each card names groups working to turn the line around.

Discreteness is real. Separateness is not.

Each value traces to the primary body named on its card, with the date it was last confirmed. Figures are refreshed on a set interval, about every six months or yearly, and sooner when a new measurement is published; for the current reading, follow the source link. When a reading changes, the new dated point is added to the tracker rather than overwriting the old, so a recovery is recorded as plainly as a decline. Any such change is noted in the card's update log. Projections are shown as dashed lines and are illustrative of the present trend, not forecasts.
