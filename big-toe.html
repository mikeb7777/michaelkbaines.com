About the book

A theory that tells you how to test it

The book starts from one idea: that information is physical, and that the universe processes it. It follows that idea through twenty-one elements, from why nothing in your hand is ever really touching anything, to gravity, quantum mechanics, the cosmic microwave background and consciousness. It keeps what physics has already confirmed: the four dimensions we observe, the Standard Model and quantum mechanics.

Where the ideas make claims about the world, the book says what measurement would settle them. Those predictions are registered in public before the data arrive, at the Ic² Research Institute, and every result is published.

New to physics? Elements 1, 2 and 3 need little background, so start there.

The book changes when the evidence does, and every change is shown. Each new version lists what was corrected, added or withdrawn, and every earlier edition stays available, so anyone can see how the thinking moved. The current version is 6E, released October 2026.

A printed edition is being prepared. The book is long, and printing it at home takes real time, paper and ink. A professionally printed copy should cost about the same, or less, and arrives bound and ready to read.
