Short bio

Michael K. Baines is a flight test engineer turned author and researcher. He writes about what the universe is made of and how connected we really are, testing both questions the way aircraft are certified. He is the author of A Quest for The Big TOE and the founder of the Ic² Research Institute.

Long bio

Michael K. Baines spent his engineering career making sure things work before people trust them with their lives. Over 39 years he moved from refrigeration systems to aircraft systems. At Boeing he taught Designer Development, training designers and engineers in Boeing’s parts systems, and designed a test parachute system for the F-22. He designed engines at Honeywell, worked on business jets for Dassault Falcon Jet and Gulfstream, spent ten years at Bombardier on the Global business jets and the clean-sheet C Series regional jet, and worked as a structural flight test engineer on the certification program for Mitsubishi’s regional jet. He later taught English at Ubon Ratchathani Rajabhat University in Thailand, and is a certified PADI diving instructor.

He now applies the same habit, that a claim is only as good as the test it survives, to larger questions. A Quest for The Big TOE, free to read, sets out an information-first approach to physics as testable predictions. He founded the Ic² Research Institute, which registers predictions before the data arrive and publishes every result. His forthcoming new edition of Rediscovering Oneness asks what the evidence actually says about human connection, keeping what holds and setting aside what does not.
