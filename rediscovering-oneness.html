About the book

How connected are we, really?

People have described a deep connection to each other and to the living world for as long as there are records. Today the idea is mostly heard from the fringe, mixed with claims that do not survive a test. This book takes the question back.

It asks what oneness is and what it is not, how human societies lived it for most of history, what physics, biology and the study of the mind actually say about it, and how the habit of separation was built. Practices with real evidence behind them are kept. Claims without it, such as raising your vibration for personal gain, are named and set aside. Open questions are left open.

It is written for the skeptical seeker and for the thoughtful believer: anyone who suspects there is something real here and wants to know how much.

This new edition replaces the first edition, which is no longer offered.
