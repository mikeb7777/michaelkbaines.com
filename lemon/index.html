A bilingual novel for Thai adults learning English · Book One in preparation

The Adventures of Lemon การผจญภัยของมะนาว

Lemon is a housekeeper in Ao Nang who freezes every time a guest asks a question that is not on her card. The book starts almost entirely in Thai and moves into English as Lemon does, so the reader learns the way she learns: from the story.

นิยายสองภาษาสำหรับคนไทยที่อยากพูดภาษาอังกฤษ เริ่มจากภาษาอังกฤษที่คุณรู้อยู่แล้ว ซ่อมทีละชิ้น แล้วพาไปข้างหน้า

About the book

Begin from what you know

Most Thai adults already speak some English, picked up from work, tourists, songs and games. The book does not throw that away. It starts with the English readers already use, fixes it one piece at a time, and moves it forward.

The story is a Thai drama with fights, gossip, a public proposal, a towel war and a food fight that gets two people banned from a restaurant. Under it are the struggles readers live with: family debt, a degree that did not lead to the job, and three attempts at English that ended in quitting.

Each chapter ends with a short lesson, a word list, questions and a writing prompt. The research behind the method is explained in the book, in plain Thai.

This page is the companion to the book. Every link Carlos gives Lemon is collected here and kept up to date.
