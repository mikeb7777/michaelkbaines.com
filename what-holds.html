What holds

Popular claims, checked

Connection, presence and the living world are real subjects, buried under claims that do not survive a test. Each claim below gets the same treatment: state it exactly, check the evidence and who paid for it, then find the real thing it points at. Where the evidence is open, it says so.

The tell. The fringe version of a claim has a before and an after, and nothing in between: no practice, no time, no cost. Real change has a middle. Alignment is trained.

From the work in progress on Rediscovering Oneness. Still to be checked: gratitude practices, heart-rate-variability biofeedback, sound healing and “528 Hz”, Reiki and energy healing, and cold exposure. Subscribers hear when new entries are added.
