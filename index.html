About

My career began in refrigeration systems, moved to aircraft refrigeration, and then to the other systems that keep an aircraft flying. I worked as a flight test structural engineer on the certification program for Mitsubishi’s regional jet, worked on business jets for Dassault Falcon Jet, Gulfstream and Bombardier, and taught engineering systems at Boeing.

Certification testing teaches one habit for life: a claim is only as good as the test it survives. That habit runs through everything I write. I founded the Ic² Research Institute so that ideas in physics, including my own, are tested in the open, with predictions registered before the data arrive and every result published.
